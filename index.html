Good vibes, high lives.

Sand Bank is built from timber, rope, brick and wicker under big shady trees. Come for a smoothie bowl in the morning, stay for tacos, burgers and the dinner buffet, then play a few rounds of table tennis on the sand.

Our newly built rooms are quiet, cool and simple, so you can sleep right where you ate.
